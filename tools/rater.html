Instructions

Each clip has four panels. Top: the original image (still) and the animation. Bottom: a zoom on the area around the part that should move, for the original and for the animation. The animation repeats every 2 seconds.

The text above the clip says which part should move and how. Watch at least two full loops. Compare the animation with the original: only the named part may move; everything else must stay exactly as in the original. Use the zoom, slow motion and frame stepping for small details.

Yes means: the named part moves as asked, and you cannot see anything else change or break. Judge only what you can see in the clip; do not worry about how it was made. No means at least one of these problems is visible:

    If the part should move but you can hardly see it move, that is "barely moves", even if nothing else is wrong. If you are not sure, give your best answer and pick Unsure.
