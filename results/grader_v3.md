## Synthetic tasks

`python3 src/synth/make_tasks.py 160` with grader v3 (results/synth_screening_v3.json, src/synth/screening_report.py):
rejected seeds 47 -> 20 (G3 19 -> 1, G5 7 -> 0, G2 7 -> 6, G4 5 -> 5, too little visible 8 -> 7); 22 seeds rejected
in v2 are now the accepted seed of their slot; tasks with occlusion >= 25%: 65 -> 74 of 160, mean occlusion 25.2% ->
28.0%. Screening now requires the task's core checks (G1-G6) on the simulated reference; G2b and G2c are admitted per
task like raster checks. Their layer-exact references reveal content behind the part that can have the part's own
colours (a dark head under a dark hat) and occlusion splits the visible part, so G2b is dropped on 52 tasks (mean
occlusion 44% vs 20% where kept) and G2c on 57. Chrome reference check: 160/160 after S009 dropped G2c (the browser
render differs from the screening simulation by a few pixels).

Synthetic mutation suite (tables above; 64 tasks: the first 40-task sample was a plain stride that hit only wave
and nod, so 4 tasks of each other motion were added; `--sample` is now stratified by motion). v3 catches more
detachments (missed thumb 25 -> 35, detach small 26 -> 33 of 64), but on flat-coloured synthetic characters three
checks are weak in both graders:
- **Center pivot 11/64 (v2 14/64).** On flat colours the joint disc hardly changes under any rotation, so G5's
  fraction is ~0 for references and broken copies alike. The fitted pivot displacement separates them
  (results/synth_g5_probe.json, src/synth/g5_probe.py): center-pivot copies move the pivot 3.5-30 px (median 10.6,
  62/65 above 4 px); references stay under 4 px except 3 of 65 (S053 5.4, S098 4.1, S104 11.5; S063's fit is not
  trusted). Making displacement a failing test (not only a rescue) is the obvious v4 change; it was not made here
  because it would re-open the synthetic screening and needs a confidence gate first.
- **Hole left 45/64 (v2 51/59).** In the synthetic hole copy the vanished part reveals the exact layers underneath, so
  the part's pixel count barely drops and v3's loss (the smaller of the fitted and the visible count) misses it. A
  model cannot reveal hidden layers, so its hole shows white or a fill and is caught; on raster tasks hole stays 21/21.
- **Gap seam 8/64, no loop 42/64:** a 2 px seam that reveals the parent's exact colour, and loops whose jump changes
  < 0.1% of a large canvas. Unchanged from v2.

## Remaining blind spots

- **G2b repaint false alarm (main source of false fails, 20 of 29 alternate runs):** a valid repaint of the
  uncovered area in a neighbouring colour (hair behind T02/T12's thumb; hair under T13's hat beyond skull.png) counts
  as a loose piece. Proposed v4 rule: skip pieces entirely inside the original footprint, with no untouched pixel and
  a flat colour; left-behind pieces are untouched, so detection would not change. Not done here (it needs another full
  re-validation); 5 more are white fill between T08's strands, 1 is T15's pale stripes.
- **Pieces hidden under the moving part:** the missed-thumb and detach-small copies on the heads (T09-T11) and jumps
  (T15, T16) leave the piece where the moving part covers it; nothing visible stays behind and no check sees the small
  moving notch. Same on T19's 6-degree veil flutter (too small a motion for G2c, G2/G2b dropped on the lace).
- **T07 no loop** (G6 0.00097 vs 0.001: the earrings are too small) and **gap seams** on T07/T14 that never split.
- **Strong squash/stretch** still fails G5 on 7 alternate runs (rigid fit cannot vouch for a stretched joint).
- **Synthetic G2b/G2c** are dropped on a third of the synthetic tasks (above): detachment on occluded synthetic parts is
  weakly covered. An occlusion-aware G2b (glue pieces through unchanged scenery inside the fitted part) is the next step.
- G2c and the G5/G3 fits are rigid: a part that legitimately bends a lot (body squash, T17/T18) drops G2c by admission.

## Open concerns

- Classification of the new alternate-run failures is one annotator's judgment (mine); the T08 white-strand cases are
  borderline and could be counted as real breaks (false fails 29 -> 24).
- Grading is slower: the rigid fits take 2-30 s per render (synthetic small or occluded parts are the slow ones).
- The synthetic screening policy changed (core checks + admission); seeds are no longer rejected for G2b/G2c.
